This website and its contact form are for general membership inquiries only. Please do not submit personal health information, medical details, urgent concerns, or emergency requests through this website.

Information We Collect

Anchor Point Medicine may collect information that you voluntarily submit through the website contact form, including your name, email address, phone number, preferred method of communication, best time to reach you, and any brief message you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website is used to respond to general inquiries, provide information about Anchor Point Medicine, and coordinate introductory conversations with individuals or families interested in membership.

Do Not Submit Medical Information

The website contact form is not intended for medical questions, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or patient care. Please do not include personal health information, symptoms, diagnoses, medications, test results, or other medical details in the form.

No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Submitting information through this website does not create a doctor-patient relationship with Dr. Speicher or Anchor Point Medicine. A doctor-patient relationship is established only after acceptance into the practice and completion of all required membership and patient documents.

Third-Party Form Processing

Anchor Point Medicine may use a third-party form service to receive and process website inquiries. Information submitted through the contact form may be transmitted through that service before being delivered to Anchor Point Medicine.

How We Protect Information

Anchor Point Medicine takes reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website. However, no website, email system, or online form can be guaranteed to be completely secure. For that reason, visitors should not submit sensitive medical information through this website.

Information Sharing

Anchor Point Medicine does not sell visitor information. Information submitted through the website may be shared only as needed to respond to inquiries, operate the website, comply with legal obligations, or support administrative functions related to the practice.

Emergency and Urgent Concerns

This website is not monitored for urgent or emergency concerns. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Changes to This Policy

Anchor Point Medicine may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
