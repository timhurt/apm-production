How billing works

Annual or semiannual billing is available at the stated annual rate. Quarterly billing is available at a modestly higher total. Monthly figures shown above are budgeting equivalents only.

Founding benefit

Founding rates are guaranteed for each member’s first two years. After that period, continuously enrolled Founding Circle members receive 10% off the then-current standard membership rate.

Health insurance still matters

Membership is not a replacement for health insurance. Members should maintain coverage for specialists, hospital and emergency care, imaging, medications, outside labs, and care not included in membership.
