How billing works

Pay annually or semiannually at the stated rate. Quarterly billing adds a nominal administrative fee. Monthly equivalents are for budgeting only.

Cancellation & refunds

Membership is annual, including installments. Cancel in writing anytime; refunds depend on unused whole months and services provided. The Patient Agreement governs.

Health insurance

Keep health insurance for specialists, hospitals, emergency care, imaging, medications, and outside labs. Membership complements your coverage; it does not replace it.

Medicare & TRICARE

Medicare and TRICARE beneficiaries are not currently eligible for membership. If you have questions about your coverage, please contact us before submitting an inquiry.

Additional costs

Some supplies, medications, vaccines, outside testing, and third-party services may cost extra. Ask about anticipated costs when reviewing the membership options for your household.
