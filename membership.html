Good to know

Health insurance

Membership does not replace health insurance. Maintain coverage for specialists, hospitals, emergency care, imaging, medications, outside labs, and other third-party services.

Medicare & TRICARE

Anchor Point does not currently accept Medicare or TRICARE beneficiaries for membership.

Additional costs

Certain supplies, medications, vaccines, outside testing, and third-party services may carry separate costs.

Cancellation & refunds

Membership is annual, even when paid in installments. Members may terminate in writing at any time. Applicable refunds depend on unused whole months in the paid period and services already provided; the Patient Agreement controls.
