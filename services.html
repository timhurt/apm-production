Care for individuals & families

One physician who understands the household.

Anchor Point is designed for the moments when a concern affects more than one chart: a spouse, a child, a parent, or the family around them. You'll have a physician who already knows the people, the medical history, and the context around the decision.

Individuals

Comprehensive primary care, prevention, chronic disease management, acute care, and care coordination for adults.

Families

Acute illness and injury care, sports and preparticipation physicals, and family medical guidance for children and adolescents, while routine well-child and preventive care remains with their pediatrician.

Services

    Proactive Health Plan

    Start with a clear picture of your health, priorities, risks, and goals—then build a plan for the year ahead.

    • Comprehensive Annual Health Review
    • Personalized recommendations with goal setting
    • Evidence-based prevention
    • Travel medicine guidance
    • Sports and pre-participation physicals
  2. 02

    Service Calls

    Timely access to evaluate, diagnose, and treat concerns directly with same-day or next-day visits for acute illness, plus secure telehealth consultations and select house calls.

    Procedures

    • Point-of-care ultrasound
    • Electrocardiogram (ECG)
    • Stitches (laceration repair)
    • Abscess drainage
    • Joint injections / aspirations
    • Ear lavage
  3. 03

    Navigating Care

    Planned checkpoints and ongoing attention to keep results, medications, and health goals moving forward.

    • Planned six-month progress review
    • Chronic disease management
    • Medication review and optimization
    • Review of outside labs and imaging
    • Ongoing attention to results and referrals
  4. 04

    Follow Ups + Referrals

    Anchor Point helps you understand what comes next and stay connected when specialists, hospitals, or other outside care are needed.

    • Specialist referrals and coordination
    • Review of outside care and results
    • Guidance before and after hospital or emergency care
    • Help navigating complex medical decisions

Not every concern needs a referral.

Dr. Speicher’s emergency-medicine background and in-office diagnostic capabilities allow many concerns to be evaluated and treated directly. When specialist or hospital care is the right next step, Anchor Point helps determine where to go and what comes next.
