Services

The breadth of care your family deserves.

Comprehensive primary care, urgent visits, prevention, chronic disease management, care coordination, and selected in-office services—all within a relationship built for continuity.

01

Primary & preventive care

Longitudinal care for adults and children, with time to understand the whole picture.

  • Comprehensive annual health assessments
  • Personalized prevention and wellness planning
  • Primary care for the whole family
  • Medication optimization
02

Responsive care

Earlier access when something changes, so you can make a clear decision about what comes next.

  • Same- or next-day appointments
  • Evaluation of acute illness and injury
  • Guidance when urgent care or emergency care may be needed
  • House calls when needed
03

Ongoing condition management

Thoughtful follow-through for chronic concerns and the decisions that accumulate over time.

  • Chronic disease management
  • Medication review and optimization
  • Review of outside labs and imaging
  • Longitudinal follow-up
04

Care navigation

A physician who helps you move through the broader healthcare system with context.

  • Specialist coordination
  • Hospital and emergency department coordination
  • Second-opinion and referral guidance
  • Travel medicine guidance
05

In-office diagnostics & procedures

Selected services can be handled directly in the practice, reducing unnecessary handoffs.

  • Point-of-care ultrasound
  • EKGs and selected diagnostic testing
  • Laceration repair and abscess drainage
  • Joint injections, ear lavage, and other selected procedures

Designed around judgment

Not every concern needs another referral.

Many issues that might otherwise lead to urgent care, an emergency department visit, or another handoff can often be evaluated in the office. When outside care is the right next step, Anchor Point helps coordinate it.
