A smaller practice. A personal check-in every month. A doctor who knows you before you need care.

Built for 250 members. On purpose.

Anchor Point Medicine is a membership-based concierge practice in Bethesda, Maryland, led by Dr. Matthew V. Speicher, a former White House physician and retired U.S. Navy commander. The practice is capped at 250 members to make room for direct access, unhurried conversations and ongoing attention to your health.

Proactive care, every month

You’ll hear from Dr. Speicher personally every month, even when you’re healthy. Care begins with a comprehensive health review and a written health plan within 48 hours, followed by a planned six-month review and ongoing attention to results, medications, referrals and preventive priorities.

The goal is to be there before you need care, with a physician who understands your medical history, your household and your priorities.

Direct access when something changes

Call or text your doctor directly. Same-day or next-day visits are available for illness and injury, with telehealth consultations and house calls when needed.

Primary care and urgent-care capabilities

Services include prevention, chronic disease management, travel medicine guidance and care coordination. In-office capabilities include stitches (laceration repair), bedside ultrasound, EKGs, joint injections and selected other procedures. When specialist care is needed, Dr. Speicher helps navigate referrals and follow-up.

Explore concierge medical services for the full scope of care, including how care for children complements their pediatrician’s routine preventive care.

Membership and health insurance

Included membership visits are not billed to insurance. Membership does not replace health insurance: you still need coverage for specialists, hospital care, imaging, prescriptions and other services outside the membership. Anchor Point does not currently accept Medicare or TRICARE beneficiaries for membership.
