Meet Dr. Speicher

Matthew V. Speicher,
DO, MBA, FACEP

Dr. Matthew V. Speicher is a Bethesda native who attended Georgetown Prep before graduating from the U.S. Naval Academy.

His Navy career took him from the cockpit of the F-14 Tomcat and F/A-18F Super Hornet to medicine, emergency medicine leadership at Walter Reed, and service as a White House physician. He returned home to establish Anchor Point.

Emergency medicine, bedside ultrasound, and procedural experience inform his acute decision-making. Anchor Point brings that breadth into a longitudinal primary-care relationship built on trust, context, and personal responsibility.

The relationship behind the practice

The physician you call “Doc.”

As a Naval aviator, Dr. Speicher experienced the value of a trusted Flight Surgeon, or “Doc,” who understood the individual, the mission, and often the family. That familiarity mattered as much as clinical judgment.

That idea is at the heart of Anchor Point: a physician relationship built before you need urgent help. Dr. Speicher created the practice to bring that continuity to individuals and families in Bethesda, with time to listen, understand the household, and stay involved as needs change.

When something important happens, you should know exactly who to call.
