About Anchor Point

Personal care.
A different model.

Dr. Matthew Speicher founded Anchor Point on the belief that medical care begins with knowing you well before you need it.

His approach was shaped by a career as a Navy commander, emergency physician, department chief at Walter Reed, and White House physician. Across each role, medicine came down to trust, sound judgment, and personal responsibility.

The practice stays small so your physician can remain accessible and provide informed, proactive care.

The practice model

Membership care.
Without insurance billing.

Traditional concierge practices often charge a membership fee alongside insurance billing for visits. Anchor Point provides included membership care directly, without insurance copays for those visits.
