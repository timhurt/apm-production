Meet Dr. Speicher

Matthew V. Speicher,
DO, MBA

Dr. Matthew V. Speicher founded Anchor Point on the belief that medical care begins with knowing you well before you need it.

His approach was shaped by a career as a Navy commander, emergency physician, department chief at Walter Reed, and White House physician. Across each role, medicine came down to trust, sound judgment, and personal responsibility.

A board-certified emergency physician and retired U.S. Navy commander, Dr. Speicher previously served as Chief of Emergency Medicine at Walter Reed and as a White House physician.

The practice stays small so your physician can remain accessible and provide informed, proactive care.

The relationship behind the practice

The physician you call “Doc.”

In Naval Aviation, “Doc” is more than a title. It is the physician you know personally and call when something happens. The flight surgeon’s role brings medical care into the life of a community, where familiarity and trust matter alongside clinical judgment.

That idea is at the heart of Anchor Point: a physician relationship built before you need urgent help. Dr. Speicher created the practice to bring that continuity to individuals and families in Bethesda, with time to listen, understand the household, and stay involved as needs change.

When something important happens, you should know exactly who to call.
