About Anchor Point

A trusted physician is
a point of continuity.

I founded Anchor Point on the belief that medical care begins with knowing you well before you need it. My approach was shaped by a career as a Navy commander, emergency physician, department chief at Walter Reed, and White House physician. Across each role, medicine came down to trust, sound judgment, and personal responsibility. I’m keeping my practice small so I can remain accessible and provide informed care when it matters most: always.

A different practice model

Visits are not billed to insurance, and a member-based model keeps the practice small for more personal, proactive care.

It offers personal access, but included visits involve no insurance billing or copays.

Do I still need insurance?

Yes. Maintain coverage for outside services, specialists, emergencies, hospital care and medications.

Can children join?

Yes. The practice welcomes adults and children.

When should I reach out?

For symptoms, results, referrals, medications or family health decisions.
