FAQ

Questions families often ask.

Is this concierge medicine?

Anchor Point shares some familiar concierge features, including a smaller practice, longer visits, easier access, and a closer physician relationship. Structurally, however, membership services are delivered outside the insurance billing model, so included visits are not billed to insurance and do not generate copays.

Do I still need health insurance?

Yes. Membership does not replace health insurance. Members should keep coverage for specialists, hospital and emergency care, imaging, medications, outside labs, and services outside the membership.

Do you care for children?

Yes. Anchor Point is designed for both adults and children, with the goal of understanding the household as well as each individual member.

What concerns should I contact you about?

Members can reach out when a symptom, test result, referral, illness, injury, medication issue, travel concern, or family health decision would benefit from thoughtful medical judgment.

What does membership include?

Membership includes primary and preventive care, direct physician access, longer visits, same- or next-day appointments, chronic disease management, care coordination, and selected office diagnostics and procedures. See Care & Services for details.

How does membership billing work?

Annual and semiannual billing are available at the stated annual rate. Quarterly billing is available at a modestly higher total. Monthly figures on the membership page are budgeting equivalents.

What is the Founding Circle?

The first 75 enrolled patients receive founding rates for their first two years. After that, continuously enrolled Founding Circle members receive 10% off the then-current standard membership rate.

How do I get started?

Inquire About Membership to start an introductory conversation and review options for you or your family before enrolling.
