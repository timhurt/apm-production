What membership makes possible

Anchor Point Medicine offers membership-based primary care for individuals and families in Bethesda, Maryland. A deliberately small practice creates room for longer visits, direct physician access, and a relationship that develops over time.

Adults and children can receive care from one physician who understands the household. Matthew V. Speicher, DO, MBA founded the practice around continuity, trust, and personal responsibility.

How is it different from concierge care?

Traditional concierge practices often combine a membership fee with insurance billing for clinical visits. Anchor Point provides included membership care outside routine insurance billing, with no copays for included visits.
